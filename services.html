01

Consultations & Checkups

A routine visit where we examine your teeth, gums, and bite to catch problems while they're still small and easy to treat. We'll walk you through anything we find in plain language — no jargon, no pressure.

  • Full visual exam of teeth, gums, and soft tissue
  • Early screening for cavities and gum disease
  • Bite and jaw alignment check
  • Personalized treatment plan if any work is needed
RecommendedEvery 6 months
Typical duration20–30 minutes
02

Cleaning & Scaling

Even with good brushing, plaque hardens into tartar that a toothbrush can't remove. Scaling clears it from above and below the gumline, and polishing leaves teeth smooth and noticeably brighter.

  • Plaque and tartar removal, above and below the gumline
  • Polishing to remove surface staining
  • Gum health assessment during the visit
  • Aftercare tips to keep buildup from returning quickly
RecommendedEvery 6 months
Typical duration30–45 minutes
03

Fillings & Cavity Treatment

When decay has broken into the tooth, we remove the damaged part and rebuild it with a tooth-coloured composite filling — restoring shape and function so you can bite and chew normally again, usually in a single visit.

  • Local anaesthesia so the procedure is pain-free
  • Removal of decayed tooth material
  • Tooth-coloured composite filling, shaped to match your bite
  • Polish and bite check before you leave
RecommendedAs soon as decay is found
Typical duration30–60 minutes
04

Root Canal Treatment

When the soft tissue inside a tooth becomes infected or inflamed — often from deep decay or an old crack — a root canal removes the damaged tissue, cleans and seals the canal, and relieves the pain, letting you keep your natural tooth rather than losing it.

  • Diagnosis to confirm the tooth needs root canal treatment
  • Removal of infected/inflamed pulp tissue under local anaesthesia
  • Cleaning, shaping, and sealing of the root canal
  • Guidance on a protective crown afterward, if needed
RecommendedWhen pain, swelling, or deep decay is present
Typical duration1–2 visits
05

Tooth Extraction

When a tooth is too damaged, decayed, or crowded to save, we remove it safely and gently under local anaesthesia — and send you home with clear aftercare instructions to heal comfortably.

  • Assessment to confirm extraction is the right option
  • Local anaesthesia for a pain-free procedure
  • Gentle, safe removal of the tooth
  • Written aftercare instructions and follow-up advice
RecommendedWhen a tooth can't be restored
Typical duration20–40 minutes
06

Gum Care

Bleeding, sensitive, or receding gums are early signs of gum disease that's easy to treat now and harder to reverse later. We assess the cause and treat it directly, often alongside a deeper cleaning.

  • Assessment of bleeding, swelling, or gum recession
  • Deep cleaning (scaling & root planing) where needed
  • Guidance on technique and products to protect your gums
  • Follow-up plan for ongoing gum health
RecommendedAt the first sign of bleeding or sensitivity
Typical duration30–45 minutes
